    Who is eligible

    • Residential households with their own roof and an electricity connection in their name.
    • Panels must be domestically manufactured (DCR) and ALMM-listed.
    • The subsidy is credited to your bank account after installation, inspection and net-metering.
    • Shops, offices and factories are not covered by this scheme.

    Scheme rules can change. Check the latest details at pmsuryaghar.gov.in or ask us during your site visit.

    Ask a Question
    How much roof space do I need?

    Plan for about 100 sq ft of shade-free roof for every 1 kW. A typical 3 kW home system needs around 300 sq ft.

    Will solar work on cloudy or rainy days?

    Yes, at a lower output. With an on-grid system, any shortfall is drawn from the grid and surplus on sunny days is exported, so it balances out over the year through net-metering.

    Does an on-grid system work during a power cut?

    No. For safety, an on-grid inverter shuts down when grid power fails. If you need backup during outages, ask us about a hybrid system with batteries.

    How long does installation take?

    Installation of a home system usually takes one to two days. Net-meter connection depends on the DISCOM's inspection schedule; we follow up on your behalf.

    When will I receive the subsidy?

    The subsidy is released after the system is installed, inspected and the net meter is connected. It is credited directly to the bank account linked in your application.

    How often should the panels be cleaned?

    Rinse the panels with plain water every two to four weeks, more often in dusty months. Avoid detergents and hard brushes. We also offer cleaning visits.

    Are loans available?

    Yes. Several banks offer rooftop solar loans for homes under the PM Surya Ghar scheme. We can guide you through the options when you apply.
